# BuildLog Design System

Version: 1.0

# Brand Identity

## Product Name

BuildLog

## Tagline

Turn today's work into tomorrow's post.

Alternative taglines:

* Log it. Share it.
* Your work deserves to be seen.
* Build. Log. Share.
* Document the journey.
* Stay consistent. Stay visible.

---

# Product Mission

BuildLog helps developers, founders, cybersecurity learners, students, designers, and creators transform daily progress into content worth sharing.

The goal is not social media automation.

The goal is helping people consistently document and share their work.

BuildLog should feel like a daily companion.

Not a marketing tool.

Not a social media dashboard.

Not an analytics platform.

---

# Brand Personality

BuildLog is:

* Encouraging
* Optimistic
* Human
* Focused
* Creative
* Thoughtful
* Minimal

BuildLog is NOT:

* Corporate
* Salesy
* Loud
* Overly playful
* Overly technical
* AI obsessed

The product should celebrate progress.

Even small wins matter.

---

# Core Product Philosophy

Every screen should answer:

"What did you build today?"

The primary user journey is:

Log Progress
→ Generate Content
→ Share
→ Repeat

Anything that does not support this loop should be questioned.

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Progress First

The user's work is the hero.

Not the AI.

Not analytics.

Not dashboards.

---

## Principle 2

Clarity Over Complexity

Users should understand every screen in less than 5 seconds.

---

## Principle 3

Whitespace Is A Feature

Do not fill space unnecessarily.

Generous spacing is required.

---

## Principle 4

Human Over Artificial

Avoid visual clichés associated with AI products.

No glowing gradients.

No futuristic holograms.

No robot illustrations.

No generic AI aesthetics.

---

## Principle 5

Focused Experiences

Every page should have one primary action.

Avoid overwhelming users.

---

# Border Radius

Small

12px

Medium

16px

Large

24px

Cards should feel soft.

Never sharp.

Never excessively rounded.

---

# Shadows

Subtle only.

Avoid heavy shadows.

Card:

0 4px 12px rgba(15,23,42,0.06)

Hover:

0 8px 24px rgba(15,23,42,0.08)

---

# Motion

Use Framer Motion.

Animation style:

Soft
Natural
Fast

Examples:

* Fade In
* Slide Up
* Slight Scale
* Hover Lift

Duration:

150ms–300ms

Avoid:

* Bounce
* Spin
* Excessive Parallax
* Infinite Animations

* Spacious
* Elevated

# Icons

Use:

Lucide Icons

---

# Illustrations

Illustrations should be custom and simple.

Avoid:

* 3D illustrations
* Cartoon characters
* Generic startup artwork

Preferred:

* Abstract workflow visuals
* Product storytelling diagrams
* Simple branded graphics

---

# Dashboard Philosophy

BuildLog is NOT a dashboard product.

Avoid:

* Excessive charts
* Vanity metrics
* Complex admin layouts

Focus on:

* Daily work logging
* Draft generation
* Progress history
* Streaks

---

# Landing Page Tone

Confident.

Simple.

Clear.

Examples:

Good:

"Turn today's work into tomorrow's post."

Good:

"Your work deserves to be seen."

Bad:

"Revolutionize your social media content generation workflow."

---

# Microcopy Guidelines

Write like a thoughtful builder.

Not a marketer.

Examples:

Good:

"What did you build today?"

Good:

"Ready to continue building?"

Good:

"Small progress compounds."

Bad:

"Unlock exponential audience growth."

Bad:

"Leverage AI-powered content optimization."

---

# Empty States

Empty states should inspire action.

Examples:

No Logs

"You built something today. Let's capture it."

No Drafts

"Your next post starts with today's work."

No History

"Every journey starts with a first log."

---

# Authentication Philosophy

Authentication should feel welcoming.

Not transactional.

Users should feel like they are joining a community of builders.

---

# Success Definition

If a screen looks like a generic AI SaaS template:

Redesign it.

If a screen feels cluttered:

Remove elements.

If users don't know the next action:

Improve hierarchy.

The BuildLog experience should feel calm, intentional, and built around one idea:

Your work deserves to be seen.
